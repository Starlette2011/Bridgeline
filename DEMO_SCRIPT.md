# Demo video script (about 2 minutes)

**0:00 Hook (15s)**
"It's 9pm. A parent gets a school notice. It's in English they can't fully read. The deadline passes, and their child misses the field trip. This happens every week to millions of families."

**0:15 Meet Bridgeline (15s)**
Open the app. "Bridgeline turns any school notice into plain language, in your own language, and tells you exactly what to do."

**0:30 Understand (30s)**
- Choose a language (for example Spanish) in the top-right
- Click the **Field trip** sample (or take a photo of a real, anonymized flyer)
- Click **Explain this notice**
- Point out: summary, urgency tag, action checklist with deadlines, money, things to bring, contact links, jargon glossary
- Click **Listen** and let a few seconds play

**1:00 Trust (15s)**
- Tick **Show original beside summary**: dates and prices are highlighted
- Show the **Double-check with the school** box and the confidence meter
- "We never hide uncertainty."

**1:15 Act (20s)**
- Click **Add everything to calendar (.ics)**
- Tick off one action and show the **My notices** agenda
- "Everything is saved only on this phone. Our server stores nothing."

**1:35 Reply and ask (15s)**
- **Reply** tab: speak or type a sentence in Spanish, click **Write the English message**, show the back-translation
- **Ask** tab: "How much does it cost?" and show the quoted sentence

**1:50 For schools (10s)**
- **For schools** tab: paste a dense message, show the reading grade drop, show translations

**2:00 Close (10s)**
"Bridgeline: so no family misses what matters. Next: a pilot with a real school and bilingual families."

## Tips
- Record in AI mode so translations show. Do a dry run first.
- Use only fictional or anonymized notices on screen.
- Show the privacy note and the AI-use disclosure in the written submission.
