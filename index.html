Free translation

Translation tries your device first (private and offline, in browsers that support it). If that is not available, short pieces of the notice are sent to MyMemory, a free online service, with emails, phone numbers, IDs and the names you listed masked first.

Adding an email raises the free daily limit of MyMemory from about 5,000 to 50,000 characters. It is stored only on this device and sent only to MyMemory.
